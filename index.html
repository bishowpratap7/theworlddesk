📋 About The World Desk — and how it works

The World Desk is a free world news aggregator focused on what matters most: wars and conflicts (Ukraine, Gaza and other global flashpoints), politics and policy (elections, diplomacy, sanctions, summits), and the global economy (trade, tariffs, energy, markets). Twice an hour, a wire scan collects the latest headlines from the public feeds of BBC News, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, DW and France 24, removes duplicates, and files them by desk.

Method, in the open: headlines and short snippets belong to their publishers — every card names its source, shows its publication time, and links to the original story. Nothing here is republished in full, and no headline is rewritten. War coverage develops fast: treat early reports as provisional and confirm at the source. The desk is edited by Bishow Pandey (@bishowppandey), a senior software & platform engineer in Kentucky, USA, who also runs The Dev Desk (developer news) and Scroll Patrol (trending posts & memes).

Frequently asked questions

What is The World Desk? A free world-news wire aggregating wars, conflicts, politics, policy and economy headlines from established outlets' public feeds, updated twice an hour.

Where does the news come from? BBC News, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, DW and France 24 — public RSS feeds, no logins, no paywall bypass. Every headline links to its source.

How fresh is it? The scan runs twice an hour; each headline carries its own publication time so you can judge for yourself.

Who runs it? Bishow Pandey — follow him on X at @bishowppandey for the live wire between updates.
